ASSISTANT:
These are the main terms you’ll need to understand and explain for your project.

| Term | Meaning in your project |
|---|---|
| **Gene expression** | How actively a gene is being used to produce RNA. Your data measure RNA counts, which do not directly measure protein amounts. |
| **Transcriptomics** | The study of RNA expression across many genes. |
| **Spatial transcriptomics** | Measuring gene expression while retaining information about where measurements came from in tissue. |
| **Visium spot** | A capture location on the tissue slide. A spot can contain material from multiple cells, so **a spot is not an individual cell**. |
| **Barcode** | An identifier linking a spot’s expression measurements to its metadata and coordinates. |
| **UMI count** | A count of captured RNA molecules identified using *unique molecular identifiers*. It helps reduce repeated counting caused by amplification. |
| **Extracellular matrix (ECM)** | The network of proteins surrounding cells that provides structural support and participates in tissue repair. |
| **Collagen** | A major structural component of the ECM. Genes such as `Col1a1`, `Col1a2`, and `Col3a1` encode collagen components. |
| **Fibrogenesis** | The process of producing fibrous connective tissue, including ECM deposition. It can occur during repair. |
| **Fibrosis** | Excessive accumulation of fibrous tissue. Elevated fibrogenic gene expression alone does not establish how much fibrosis exists. |
| **Marker gene** | A gene used as an indicator of a cell type or biological process. A marker is evidence, not a definitive diagnosis. |
| **Hepatocyte** | The main functional liver cell, involved in metabolism, protein production, and processing drugs. |
| **Hepatic stellate cell** | A liver cell that can become activated after injury and contribute to ECM production. |
| **Liver zonation** | Differences in cell activity across regions of a liver lobule. The **pericentral region**, or Zone 3, surrounds the central vein; the **periportal region** is near portal structures. |
| **Necrosis** | Cell death caused by injury. A necrotic region and a region actively producing ECM describe different biological features. |

For interpreting the calculations, these terms are equally important:

- **Normalization:** Adjusting counts for differences in the total RNA captured per spot. Otherwise, a spot might appear more active simply because more RNA was measured.
- **Upregulation:** Higher gene expression relative to a stated comparison. Always specify “higher than what?”
- **Biological replicate:** An independently sampled biological unit—here, a mouse. Many spots from one mouse do not replace having more mice.
- **Annotation:** An assigned label, such as `Fibrogenic_spot`. Its meaning depends on how it was created.
- **Fibrogenic score:** A mathematical summary of selected marker expression. Its interpretation depends on the genes, normalization, and reference we choose.
- **Validation:** Checking whether a calculation behaves correctly and whether its biological interpretation is supported. Checking a score against labels created from the same genes provides limited independent evidence.

The distinction to keep in mind throughout is: **your project measures RNA expression associated with tissue repair; it does not directly measure collagen protein, scar tissue, or changes within the same cells over time.**
