## All project deliverables, from the beginning

The project now asks two linked questions. **Which genes should we investigate as potential targets after acute liver injury?** And **can we use compound structure to predict one laboratory measurement for one of those targets?** The first question produces a ranked set of biological hypotheses. The second produces an evaluated prediction of assay activity. Published experiments connect the two and help decide which hypotheses deserve follow-up.

### Checkpoint 1 · Data audit, collagen score, and figures

### Checkpoint 2 · Full-data spatial analysis, ranking engine, and local app

The full workflow recovered **17,370 spots across six mice**, with two mice each at 24, 48, and 72 hours. The study used a necrotic-region annotation at 24 hours and collagen-linked annotations at 48 and 72 hours. Each Visium spot can contain RNA from multiple cells. The comparison is between annotated and other spots **within the same injured mouse**.

For each gene, the engine normalizes the spot count as `ln(1 + 10,000 × count / spot library total)`. It then subtracts mean expression in other spots from mean expression in annotated spots, separately for each mouse. At each later time point it takes the smaller of the two mouse differences, floors it at zero, and transforms it using `x/(1+x)`. For example, differences of `+0.8` and `−0.2` give that time point zero contribution. The 48- and 72-hour contributions are averaged into the spatial component. The 24-hour data stay available as separate context.

The gene ranking combines **60% spatial evidence, 25% small-molecule tractability, and 15% human hepatic-fibrosis association**. Tractability summarizes evidence that small molecules can engage the mapped human target. The fibrosis association adds chronic disease context to an acute injury dataset. A complete rank requires high-confidence one-to-one mouse–human mapping, reproducible positive spatial evidence at a later time, and the required external components. Of **32,285 features**, **4,789** passed the mapping gate and **2,248** entered the complete ranking. The spatial-only screen and all-feature audit preserve evidence and exclusion reasons for features outside the complete ranking.

The checked deliverables are the spatial summaries, ranking tables, provenance manifests, evidence profiles, and local Streamlit app with lookup, maps, and downloads. The repository checklist still leaves course submission confirmation open for this checkpoint.
